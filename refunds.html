Refunds

Refunds and returns

Nothing is on sale yet, so nothing can be refunded yet. This is the policy that will apply from the first day we sell, written now so it is not improvised later. This page is in English for now; a Hebrew version will follow.

Cancelling a distance purchase

Under Israel's Consumer Protection Law, 5741-1981, a purchase made online can be cancelled within fourteen days of receiving the goods, or of receiving the transaction details, whichever is later. When we sell, we will honour that in full: write to us within the period, and we will refund the price within fourteen days of the cancellation, less any charge the law allows.

Food and supplements once opened

The law excludes goods that are perishable, or that were sealed for health or hygiene reasons and have been unsealed, from the right of cancellation. Most of what we plan to sell is one or the other. So: a sealed, unopened product can be returned within the period for a full refund; an opened jar of date honey or bottle of oil cannot, unless it is faulty.

Faulty or damaged goods

If something arrives damaged, spoiled, or not as described, tell us within a reasonable time with a photograph and the lot number from the pack. We will replace it or refund it, our choice, and cover the return. Every batch will carry a lot number and a published certificate of analysis, so a fault can be traced.

How refunds will be paid

To the payment method used for the purchase, within fourteen days of our confirming the cancellation or the fault.
