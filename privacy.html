What this site collects on its own

Nothing. There is no analytics script, no advertising pixel, no social-media embed and no third-party font or script loaded from another company. Every file the page needs is served from the site itself.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub's servers may record the IP address and browser details of each request in their own logs for security and operations. We do not receive or use those logs. GitHub's handling of that data is described in GitHub's privacy statement.

One preference is stored in your browser: which language you chose, English or Hebrew. It is kept in your browser's local storage under the name eyl_lang, never leaves your device, and is removed when you clear the site's data. It is not a cookie and it is not used to identify you.

What the forms collect

Two forms exist: a waitlist that asks for an email address, and a contact form that asks for a name, an email address and a message. Each is shown only when it is connected to a service that can actually receive it; when a form is hidden, nothing is collected.

When a form is live, what you type is sent to a form-processing service (Formspree, Inc.) which delivers it to us by email and holds a copy in its own system, subject to its own privacy policy. We use what you send for one purpose only: to reply to you, or, for the waitlist, to tell you once when we open for sale. We will not add you to anything else, share the address, or sell it.

You can withdraw at any time by writing to us through the contact page; we will delete the address from the service and from our mail.

Legal basis and your rights

We process what you send on the basis of your consent, given when you press send. Israeli law (the Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981) applies; where the GDPR applies to you, the same rights are honoured. You may ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Write to us through the contact page and we will answer within thirty days.

Links to other sites

Pages on this site link to sources — Sefaria for traditional texts, journal articles by their DOI — and those sites have their own privacy practices. Following a link takes you off this site.

Children

The site is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect anything from anyone under eighteen.

Changes

If we ever add anything that collects more than this — analytics, a shop, a newsletter platform — this page will change first, and where consent is required we will ask before anything is set.
