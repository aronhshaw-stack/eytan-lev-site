Cookies

Cookies

The short answer is that there are none. Here is the long answer. This page is in English for now; a Hebrew version will follow.

Cookies we set

None. The site does not set a cookie of any kind, first-party or third-party.

What is stored instead

One value in your browser's local storage, named eyl_lang, remembering whether you chose English or Hebrew. It is strictly functional: without it the site would ask you to choose your language on every page. It is never sent to us or to anyone, and it contains nothing but the word en or he. You can remove it by clearing the site's data in your browser.

Third parties

Fonts and the 3D library the home page uses are served from this site, not from Google or a CDN, so opening a page here does not contact any other company. There is no analytics service, no advertising network and no social-media widget.

If you send a form, what you type goes to a form-processing service as described in the privacy policy. That happens only when you press send, and it sets nothing in your browser.

Why there is no cookie banner

Consent banners exist to ask permission for cookies and similar storage that are not strictly necessary for the service you asked for. This site has none of those, so there is nothing to ask permission for, and a banner would be theatre. If that changes — if we add analytics, say — we will ask before setting anything, and this page will say so.
